# Assets and licenses

Everything the client ships is either original to this project or listed here with its license.
There are no copyrighted characters, logos or game art, and nothing is loaded from a CDN.

bundled.

## Not client assets

The spoken voices are generated by the server through Gnani Timbre and streamed to the client;
the client discloses on screen that they are AI-generated. Question packs live in `server/`.
